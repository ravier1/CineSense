# CineSense brand direction

## Logo

The mark combines:

- A cinematic “C” for CineSense
- A play triangle for watching
- A small green status light for live activity

The SVG mark is available at `apps/web/public/brand/cinesense-mark.svg` and is also used as the app icon.

## Color palette

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0B0A0D` | Main canvas and cinematic dark mode |
| Panel | `#15131A` | Cards and surfaces |
| Plum | `#17131B` | Logo background and deep contrast |
| Warm gold | `#F2B56B` | Primary accent, buttons, ratings, focus |
| Light gold | `#FFCF8A` | Hover states and highlights |
| Ivory | `#F8F6F1` | Main text and logo play triangle |
| Muted grey | `#AAA8AC` | Secondary text |
| Live green | `#9BD7B0` | Current watching, success, connected states |

## Voice

Warm, personal, observant, and slightly cinematic. The product should feel like a beautiful notebook kept by someone who loves films—not like a streaming dashboard.

## UI direction

- Dark-first, with generous spacing and rounded cards.
- Posters and stills provide visual texture, but text remains easy to read.
- Gold represents attention and intention: ratings, plans, and primary actions.
- Green is reserved for real-time states, so it remains meaningful.
- Avoid aggressive social metrics; emphasize memories, context, and conversation.
